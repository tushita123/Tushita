Library

Tushita Centre Delhi has an incredible collection of books gathered over decades - from rare transcripts of teachings to the newest publications of teachers such as His Holiness the Dalai Lama and Lama Zopa Rinpoche. If you wish to learn the basics of meditation, take your Buddhist studies to a deeper level, or learn a brand new topic such as Buddhist Debate and Logic, you are likely to find resources in our library! Come check out the library in-person at our meditation centre in the heart of Hauz Khas, New Delhi - and browse the entire collection online as well!

Browse Books Online

We also warmly welcome donations of any Buddhist literacy resources that may be collecting dust in your homes! We love to give old books a new home, and help many people benefit from them.

To donate books or other resources, contact us by email or WhatsApp!
